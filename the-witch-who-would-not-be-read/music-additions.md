# Music additions (12 new cues)

All files are in `audio/`, 44.1 kHz stereo 128 kbps MP3, trimmed to 38-75 s with a 3 s fade-out and 0.3 s fade-in, loudness-matched to about -16 LUFS (true peak -1.5 dBTP). Entries are appended to `audio/cue-manifest.json` (existing 9 entries untouched). All new tracks are by Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0, so the credits below are required.

**Heads-up:** moods were chosen from the catalogue's titles and descriptions and the pit-band context, not by ear. Audition each once before wiring.
