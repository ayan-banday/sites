The short answer

Everything you say is saved raw first, then cut into pieces that land as log lines, ticket ideas or fog. Work happens only in tickets, and you hold two gates: you mark a question ready, and you resolve the answer. Between those gates an agent works alone, mostly at night, so your day goes to deciding and the AI's night goes to doing.

Where does each part of your life sit in Fuji, from the summit down to a ticket?

Three levels: the summit, a map, a ticket. Every map climbs toward the summit, and every ticket moves one map.

The tree is the real folder layout. The ringed branch is where daily work happens.

  • summit.md Mount Olympus, the $1B company. Links every map.
    • ranges/ Udyaan, Business, Self. Maps with a destination.
      • a map/ map.md, log.md, fog.md, tickets/
    • plateau/ Body and Network. A standard to hold.
    • dormant/ Maps quiet for 3 weeks.
    • fog.md Ideas with no map yet.
    • inbox/ Your words, as said.
    • gear/ Plain Python scripts.

A range holds maps that finish: each has a destination and is done when it gets there. The plateau holds what never finishes, sleep, food, health and people, so it carries a standard instead, and a slipping standard opens a ticket.

Dormant is where a map goes when nothing has touched it for 3 weeks. A script moves it on Mondays, never the AI, so a pause is a fact you can see and the summit only lists live work.

The three rule: no folder holds more than three maps. A fourth nests under one of them or waits in dormant.

The three rule exists because a fourth live map splits attention more than it adds progress. It forces the choice early.

What happens to a reflection after you say it to Claude?

It's saved word for word in inbox/, then split into pieces, and each piece goes to the one place it belongs.

The sheet at the top is the raw file. Each cord is one piece; its socket is where that piece lands.

"Woke at 3, used the phone, had an idea about onboarding."

Raw first, because routing is a guess and your words are the evidence. If a piece lands wrong, the original is still there to route again, and every routed line points back to it.

Facts become log lines. Sleep, food, health and people go to a plateau log as dated lines, with the date the thing happened, not the day you said it.

Thoughts about a map go into a ticket's Ideas or the map's fog.md. An idea with no map goes to the root fog.md with a line of context, so it still makes sense in a month.

Duplicates are the main risk. The Oct 22 run showed it: some days were already routed once, so each log is checked before a line goes in.

How does a Fuji ticket move from a question to a decision, and who moves it?

Five statuses, and you own the two that need judgment: ready and resolved.

Each box is a status. Its lane says who sets it. The line is one ticket's path.

  1. open: a question exists. You or a session with you writes it, from your words.

  2. ready: you say the Question and Ideas are complete enough for an agent to work alone.

  3. claimed: one session, or the night run, takes the ticket so nobody else does.

  4. review: the agent has drafted the Answer and stops. You read it.

  5. resolved: you, or a session working with you, writes the decision into the map.

The gates sit where taste lives. An agent can't tell whether a question is the right one, and it can't decide what an answer means for your map, so it never sets ready or resolved. Resolving writes one line to the map's Decisions so far, moves the ticket in the map's list and updates "Left off here", so a paused map restarts from that line alone.

What runs in Fuji while you sleep, and what does it touch?

Every night at 12:47 am the night run works each ready research or task ticket whose blockers are resolved, and commits to main.

Each dot is a night with a night run. The ring on Monday is the weekly dormant check at 8 am.

It skips prototype and grilling tickets, even when they're ready, because those need you in the room.

Builds stay inside Fuji. A deck or a page goes in the map's output/; anything that changes the world, a message, a sign-up, a payment, comes back to you as a checklist in the Answer.

The last commit message is your morning note: one line per ticket, what you must decide, and what stayed ready and why.

It runs at night because watching an agent work pulls you into it. You plan in the day; it works while you're asleep.

Why does every write to a map leave a log line, and what do the gear scripts check?

Time is how you measure growth, so every write appends one timed line to the map's log.md, and plain scripts do the repeatable work for free.

Each row is one script in gear/: what it does, then what it gives you.

ScriptWhat it doesWhat it gives you
log.pyAdds one line with the real time to a map's log.md, newest at the bottom.A record of when each decision happened, and the signal the dormant check reads.
check-maps.pyChecks every ticket header, blocker, list, log, link and the three rule, then prints each map's frontier.A PASS before every push, so a broken map never reaches main.
dormant.pyOn Mondays at 8 am, moves maps with no log line for 21 days into dormant/.A summit that lists only live work, with no AI judging what's quiet.

Scripts cost no tokens and give the same result every time. The AI only does what needs judgment: splitting a reflection, drafting an answer. Anything with a rule goes to a script.

What stops an agent in Fuji from spending tokens on the wrong things?

Four rules keep tokens on what moves your maps. The first was written after unasked research on Oct 22.

Each card shows the rule, then the slip it prevents.

DoResearch only on your say-so, or on a ticket you marked ready. Other ideas get listed in one line each for your yes.
PreventsUnasked research on side topics, like the food and meds drafts on Oct 22.
DoOne ticket, one session. Each approved ticket runs in its own cloud session, touching only its own files.
PreventsOne chat that runs several tickets and mixes their files and logs.
DoRead Charlotte, never edit it. Fuji copies what it needs and cites the path.
PreventsTwo systems drifting, with nobody sure which copy is true.
DoSend learning to Shasta. Fuji links to what you studied there.
PreventsStudy notes piling up in maps that exist to finish things.
