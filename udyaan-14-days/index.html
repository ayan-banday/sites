14Days

What happens in Udyaan's 14 days?

This is the simpler plan, updated after your feedback. Tap the page to tear it off.

0Day

We collect each student's details.

Students fill one form. That is all we need.

1–3Days

Each student picks a farm problem.

They pick one their subject can solve, from the campus chit sheet.

Computer scienceSpot a sick crop from one photo.
CommerceTell a farmer the best day to sell.
ScienceWarn a field before it runs dry.
4Day

We help students form teams.

Each team agrees on one problem.

one team, one problem
5–8Days

Teams build once a mentor says go.

They post updates on the dashboard.

9–10Days

A mentor checks the work.

Teams hear what works and what to fix.

11–13Days

Teams fix what the mentor flagged.

This is the last stretch before demo day.

14DayDemo day

Each team gives a 3-minute demo.

Then we give awards.
