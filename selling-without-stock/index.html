The store lives on the gap

A customer pays $80 for a pair of shoes. The supplier charges the store $50. The $30 in between is the store's to keep.

That gap is called the markup.

Amazon owns the stock. A dropshipper owns none.

What the 10 do differently

The winners get found

When thousands of stores sell the same product, customers have to find you. So the winners pay to be seen.

Good marketing is the main thing that separates thriving stores from forgotten ones.

A product is easy to copy. A trusted brand is not.

Fashion Nova

Case: Fashion Nova

Grew by putting its clothes in the feeds where young people already spend their time.

Fashion Nova keeps its own stock, so it is not a dropshipper. It shows what marketing can do.

ITB-01Wrap-up

Three things to take home

  1. 1

    What it is

    Selling products you never hold. A fast-growing, multi-billion-dollar trend.

  2. 2

    Why people do it

    Cheaper, lower risk and quicker to start than keeping stock like Amazon.

  3. 3

    Why it is hard

    The same ease brings crowds, slow shipping and thin margins. The winners build a brand people trust.
